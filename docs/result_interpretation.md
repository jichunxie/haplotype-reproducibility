# Result interpretation and claim boundaries

- HaploPerturb's public and donor-panel rank-one backgrounds are highly concordant on shared
  coordinates. This does not establish equal sequence-to-function effects.
- The finite-panel simulation evaluates recovery under known model-generated truth. Search
  certification concerns the fixed quadrature distribution, while model adequacy is assessed
  separately.
- The eQTL benchmark measures ranking enrichment against Fujita et al.'s published two-step-FDR
  indicator. It is not individual eQTL discovery, a causal-variant analysis, or directional
  validation.
- The paper supports population-informed haplotype sequence input over a lead-only reference edit.
  The v75 comparison does not establish superiority of HaploPerturb rank one over the empirical-mode
  or LD-sign backgrounds.
- Bootstrap intervals resample whole loci and summarize finite-locus variability; they do not
  incorporate AlphaGenome server-model uncertainty or uncertainty from selecting the GWAS loci.
